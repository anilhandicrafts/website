About Anil Handicrafts

Agra's Premier Shopping Destination

Located right across the prestigious Hotel ITC Mughal on Fatehabad Road, Anil Handicrafts is a sprawling shopping emporium spread across 6,000 sq. feet and 3 floors. Since our establishment, we have been the trusted choice for tourists and art lovers visiting the city of the Taj Mahal.

One-Stop Shopping Experience

Showcasing an extensive range of authentic Indian products under one roof, we offer our clients the convenience of satisfying all their shopping needs in a single location. With ample parking space and products thoughtfully organized across various sections, we cater to large tour groups and individual travelers alike.

What Makes Us Unique

We are the only store in Agra where customers can witness live demonstrations of exquisite miniature Mughal paintings created by a National Award-winning artist. This exclusive experience allows visitors to appreciate the intricate artistry that defines our heritage.

Approximately 40% of our products are handcrafted in-house by our skilled artisans, ensuring authenticity and superior quality. We have pioneered several innovations in the fields of textiles and handicrafts, continually setting new standards in traditional craftsmanship.

Custom Creations & Expert Service

We specialize in creating customized dresses with an extensive range of premium fabrics, tailored to your exact specifications. Our customer-friendly, multilingual sales team ensures a comfortable shopping experience for visitors from around the world.

With long-lasting partnerships with leading names in the tourist industry and a reputation built on trust, quality, and authenticity, Anil Handicrafts continues to be the preferred destination for those seeking genuine Indian handicrafts and textiles.

Kids Collection

Stylish clothing for boys and girls including beautiful lehengas, traditional wear, and contemporary outfits for children.

Miniature Paintings & Fine Art

Mughal Miniature Paintings, Radha Krishna panels in sandalwood, Natraj in silver-clad wood — with live demonstrations by a National Award-winning artist.

Sacred & Divine Collection

Handcrafted marble murtis — Ganesha, Shiva, Hanuman, Durga Maa, Saraswati, Sai Baba & Radha Krishna — ethically sourced Makrana marble, cast bronze & marble dust.

Curator's Treasure

Rare collector's pieces — Dokra Ram Sawari, silver-clad Natraj, Beharupiya Ganesh in Bastar art, Royal Elephant in marble, and intricate sandalwood sculptures.

Home Decor & Furnishings

Brass sculptures, bonded bronze figurines, Kalpavriksha Radha Krishna, Buddha statues, bedsheets, cushion covers, quilts, embroidery & premium fabrics.

Tea & Spices

Authentic Indian tea blends and aromatic spices sourced from the finest regions of India for connoisseurs worldwide.
